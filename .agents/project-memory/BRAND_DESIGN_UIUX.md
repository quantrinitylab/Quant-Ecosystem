# Brand, Design, and UI/UX

## Brand architecture

- Masterbrand: `QUANTRINITY`.
- Company/ecosystem domain: `quantrinity.in`.
- Consumer identity/mail domain: `quantmail.in`.
- Endorsed product pattern: `QuantMail — by QUANTRINITY`.

## Mark direction

Original continuous infinity-ribbon concept with restrained India-inspired saffron/light/green spectrum and navy detail. Do not copy Meta geometry or trade dress. Final geometry, one-color/reversed versions, tiny-size behavior, and cultural review require editable visual work.

## Design principles

1. Original, not derivative.
2. Outcome is the interface.
3. AI belongs inside the workflow.
4. One family with distinct products.
5. Dark-first, not dark-only.
6. Accessibility by construction.
7. Design before migration.
8. Evidence before rollout.
9. Labels cannot outrun capability.

## Landed QuantMail design work

- Semantic light/dark/high-contrast roles.
- Auth and shell identity bridges.
- Dark-shell adoption across core surfaces.
- Navigation grouping.
- Recovery and activation states.
- Compose/thread/copilot hierarchy improvements.
- Live labels, signature, and vacation responder.
- Removal of fake persistence and unsupported shortcut claims.
- Safer global loading/error recovery.

## Capability-truth examples

- Say `Search mail`, not `Search mail and people`, until retrieval/results include people.
- Say `Schedule draft`, not `Schedule send`, until durable delivery exists.
- Say local file selection, not upload, until object persistence and verification exist.
- Keep unavailable settings read-only until their backend contract is real.
- AI generation must support preview/apply/cancel and must not silently overwrite drafts.

## Figma state

Repository design documents and handoff artifacts remain the operational reference. Earlier Figma capability exposed a View seat rather than a reliable full-edit workflow. A complete editable screen-by-screen file is still missing.

## Visual acceptance still required

- Mastermark and endorsed lockups.
- Desktop/tablet/mobile variants.
- Light/dark/high-contrast parity.
- Empty/loading/error/offline/permission states.
- WCAG AA contrast, focus visibility, names, landmarks, reduced motion, and touch targets.
- Visual regression at agreed breakpoints.
- No raw runtime/service failure copy.
