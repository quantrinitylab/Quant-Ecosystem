# M38 — Quanty in QuantCalendar UX

## Read assistance
Summarize schedule, identify conflicts, find open windows, explain event changes, extract meeting details from authorized context, find related Mail/Drive/Contacts.

## Planning assistance
Propose event title, propose duration, propose candidate times, prepare RSVP, prepare reschedule, prepare follow-up, prepare agenda.

## Approval
Quanty may prepare a scheduling action but cannot silently book, RSVP, cancel, move, invite, or change reminders. Actions that affect other people or external state display impact before approval.

## UX placement
Quanty suggestions appear in event/scheduling context. Global Quanty remains available from the shell. Do not obscure the time grid with a permanent chat panel.

## Truth boundary
Availability is authoritative only when returned by Calendar/provider policy. Model guesses are labeled as suggestions.
