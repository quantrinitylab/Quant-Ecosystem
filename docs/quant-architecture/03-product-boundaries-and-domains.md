# 03 — Product Boundaries and Domains

QuantMail owns identity integration, mailboxes, messages, threads, labels, contacts, calendars, Drive/Docs surfaces and Git/CodeHub integration.
QuantChat owns conversations, messages, channels, communities, presence, calls, meetings and delivery state.
QuantAI owns AI conversations, model sessions, routing, agents, plans, runs, approvals, memory references, artifacts and automations.
QuantGram owns profiles, follows, posts, stories, media, comments, reactions and feed projections.
QuantWave owns profiles, posts, replies, communities, follows, trends and moderation state.
QuanTube owns channels, video, audio, playlists, live sessions, subscriptions, watch state and creator monetization.
QuantMax owns profiles, discovery candidates, preferences, matches, short video, live and safety state.
QuantCooks owns projects, assets, timelines, edits, renders, generated media, templates and creator marketplace.
QuantAds owns advertisers, campaigns, creatives, audiences, bids, auctions, spend, attribution, payouts and fraud decisions.

QuantTrinity owns economy policy only: credit value, free allowance, plan catalog, commission and overage defaults. It does not become a hidden owner of product data.

No product may directly mutate another product's source-of-truth tables.
