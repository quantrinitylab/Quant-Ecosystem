# M18 — QuantMail Cost & Capacity Architecture

## Definition of done
- Cost domains are identified.
- Unit economics are measurable.
- Storage and bandwidth have separate cost paths.
- Search and Quanty usage are metered by workload.
- Autoscaling uses dependency-aware signals.
- Capacity tiers and isolation thresholds are documented.
- 100K/1M/10M/100M scenarios have explicit modeling inputs.
- FinOps dashboards and anomaly response are defined.
- Cost optimization cannot bypass correctness, security, or product policy.
- No hyperscale claim is treated as production evidence without measured validation.
