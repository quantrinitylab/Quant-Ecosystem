# M24 — QuantMail Enterprise & Organization Platform

## Goal
Provide enterprise-grade organization management without turning the organization layer into a replacement for product ownership.

## Enterprise layers
Organization → membership → identity federation → domains/groups → delegated administration → organization policy → product administration.

## Principles
1. Organization membership is not product permission by itself.
2. Product domains remain owners of product policy.
3. Identity federation authenticates; authorization remains scoped.
4. Organization policies can restrict capabilities but cannot grant capabilities the platform does not allow.
5. Enterprise automation must be auditable and reversible where possible.
