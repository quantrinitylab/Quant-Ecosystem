# M35 — Unified Communications Platform

## Goal
Define shared communication infrastructure for email, push, SMS/voice where applicable, in-app notifications, and transactional messaging without collapsing product ownership.

## Surfaces
- Message delivery center
- Notification preferences
- Channel status
- Templates
- Delivery analytics
- Suppression
- Provider health
- Communication audit

## Principles
Product domains own meaning; Communications owns transport and delivery orchestration.
Security, legal, and transactional messages have higher policy priority than growth messaging.
