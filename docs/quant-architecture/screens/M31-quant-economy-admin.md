# M31 — Economy Administration & Finance Governance

## Goal
Define the operator-facing control plane for plans, pricing, entitlements, credits, payments, refunds, promotions, reconciliation, and financial risk.

## Surfaces
- Economy overview
- Plans/pricing
- Subscription operations
- Credit ledger investigation
- Usage/metering
- Invoices
- Refunds
- Disputes
- Promotions
- Provider health
- Reconciliation
- Finance audit
- Emergency controls

## Principle
This is Economy-owned administration, not a global god-admin. Product admins remain responsible for product policy.
