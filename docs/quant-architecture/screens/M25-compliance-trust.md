# M25 — QuantMail Compliance & Trust

## Goal
Define a verifiable compliance and customer-trust layer around the existing security, privacy, lifecycle, and enterprise architecture.

## Principles
1. Compliance claims require evidence.
2. Legal policy does not replace technical authorization.
3. Customer-facing trust information is versioned.
4. Processor and subprocessor changes are governed.
5. Legal holds override ordinary deletion where lawfully applicable.
6. Compliance evidence minimizes customer content.

## Surfaces
Internal compliance operations, customer compliance center, Trust Center, DPA/subprocessor information, audit evidence, legal requests, and transparency reporting.
