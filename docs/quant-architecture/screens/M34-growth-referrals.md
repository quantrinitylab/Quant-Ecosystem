# M34 — Growth, Referrals & Lifecycle

## Goal
Define acquisition, onboarding, referrals, invitations, retention, and re-engagement without allowing growth systems to bypass trust, privacy, security, or Economy controls.

## Surfaces
- Welcome/onboarding
- Referral center
- Invite flow
- Referral status
- Rewards
- Lifecycle messaging
- Re-engagement
- Growth analytics
- Abuse review

## Principles
Growth incentives are policy-controlled economic effects. Consent and communication preferences remain authoritative.
