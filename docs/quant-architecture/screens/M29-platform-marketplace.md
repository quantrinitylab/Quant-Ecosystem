# M29 — Platform Marketplace & Monetization

## Goal
Define a safe marketplace for first-party and approved third-party capabilities across the Quant ecosystem.

## Surfaces
- Marketplace discovery
- App/integration detail
- Developer publishing
- Pricing and trial
- Installation/authorization
- Entitlement management
- Reviews/reporting
- Revenue and payout reporting
- Admin moderation

## Principles
- Marketplace listing is not automatic trust.
- Installation grants scoped capability, not unrestricted account access.
- Economy owns commercial primitives; marketplace domain owns listing/install lifecycle.
- Developer APIs remain governed by M23.
- Security/trust review remains authoritative for high-risk integrations.
