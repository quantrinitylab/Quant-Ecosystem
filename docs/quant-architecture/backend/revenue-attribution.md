# Revenue Attribution

Revenue attribution maps financial records to product family, plan, package, promotion, region, and acquisition context where legally and technically valid.

Rules:
- attribution dimensions are versioned
- invoice/payment remains financial source of truth
- attribution never rewrites invoices
- shared-platform costs remain distinguishable from product revenue
- missing attribution is explicit UNKNOWN, never guessed

QuantAds revenue and creator payouts remain separate accounting domains even when they use shared Economy primitives.
