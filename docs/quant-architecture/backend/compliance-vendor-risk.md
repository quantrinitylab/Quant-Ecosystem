# M25 — Vendor Risk

## Assessment
Evaluate security, privacy, availability, residency, subprocessors, incident history, contract terms, and business continuity.

## Risk tiers
Critical, high, medium, low based on data sensitivity and operational dependency.

## Lifecycle
Candidate → assessed → approved/restricted → monitored → reassessed → retired.

Vendor risk feeds M18 cost/capacity, M20 security, M21 privacy, and M22 residency decisions.
