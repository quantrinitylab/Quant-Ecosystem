# M24 — Organization Domain

## Ownership
Organization Platform owns organization identity, membership relationships, organizational roles, invitations, groups, organization lifecycle, and enterprise-level policy references.

It does not own Mail messages, Calendar events, Drive files, or product-specific policy tables.

## Organization states
PROVISIONING → ACTIVE → RESTRICTED → SUSPENDED → CLOSING → CLOSED.

## Membership
A membership links a principal to an organization with status, role references, join source, timestamps, and lifecycle metadata.

A principal may belong to multiple organizations.
