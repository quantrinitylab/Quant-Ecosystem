# M25 — DPA & Privacy Terms

## Architecture boundary
Legal documents describe contractual obligations; technical enforcement remains in Identity, Privacy, Security, Lifecycle, Residency, and product domains.

## DPA metadata
Track version, effective date, applicable regions, customer acceptance/association, processor categories, and superseded versions.

## Changes
Material changes follow controlled publication and customer notification workflows where required.

The system must not infer legal consent merely from an authenticated session.
