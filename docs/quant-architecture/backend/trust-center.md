# M25 — Public Trust Center

## Public content
Publish current security/privacy practices, subprocessor information, certifications/attestations, availability/security commitments, and contact paths.

## Versioning
Every published artifact has version, publication date, scope, and status.

## Truthfulness
Marketing copy cannot claim certification, encryption mode, residency, or security behavior that lacks verified technical/legal evidence.

Expired certifications are clearly marked rather than silently presented as current.
