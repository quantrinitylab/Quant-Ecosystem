# Economy Fraud Controls

## Signals
- payment velocity
- failed payment patterns
- unusual credit purchase/spend
- refund velocity
- device/account changes
- promotion abuse
- multiple-account correlation
- chargeback history
- entitlement exploitation

Signals are risk inputs, not verdicts.

## Actions
ALLOW, STEP_UP, HOLD, LIMIT, REVIEW, BLOCK.

High-impact actions require policy and audit. Automated controls must have bounded scope, expiry, reason codes, and appeal/review paths.

Security systems own account compromise; Economy owns transaction risk and economic abuse. Cross-domain actions use explicit contracts.
