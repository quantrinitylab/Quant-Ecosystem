# Economy Analytics

Track:
- active plans
- conversion
- retention
- credit purchase
- credit utilization
- free-tier exhaustion
- overage
- refund rate
- chargeback rate
- revenue by product
- cost-to-serve by product
- entitlement denial rate

Analytics are derived from Economy events and financial records, not ad-hoc product counters.

Financial reporting separates recognized revenue concepts from operational usage metrics and must not infer accounting treatment without the finance policy layer.
