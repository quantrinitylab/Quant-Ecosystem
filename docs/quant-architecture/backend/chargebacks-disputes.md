# Chargebacks & Disputes

## Lifecycle
RECEIVED → REVIEWING → EVIDENCE_PREPARING → SUBMITTED → WON/LOST

Chargebacks are not ordinary refunds.

## Evidence
Evidence references invoice, payment, entitlement history, usage, delivery state, and policy-relevant records without exposing unnecessary personal data.

## Effects
A dispute may trigger temporary entitlement review or credit restriction. Such actions are policy-controlled, scoped, reversible where possible, and audited.

A lost dispute creates explicit financial adjustment records; historical transactions are never rewritten.
