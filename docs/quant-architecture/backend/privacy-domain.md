# M21 — Privacy Domain

## Ownership
Privacy platform owns privacy policy metadata, consent/choice records where applicable, data-use declarations, privacy requests, and privacy audit decisions.

Product domains remain owners of their actual user data.

## Request lifecycle
REQUESTED → VALIDATING → AUTHORIZING → EXECUTING → VERIFYING → COMPLETED.

Privacy operations include access/export, correction where supported, restriction, deletion, and consent/choice changes.

## Rules
A privacy preference is not permission to access another user's data. Authorization remains owned by Identity/security/product policy.
