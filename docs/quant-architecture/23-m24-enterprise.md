# M24 — QuantMail Enterprise & Organization Platform

## Definition of done
- Organization lifecycle is defined.
- Membership and roles are scoped.
- OIDC/SAML SSO is defined.
- SCIM provisioning is idempotent and safe.
- Enterprise domains and groups are defined.
- Delegated admin has explicit boundaries.
- Enterprise policy is versioned and auditable.
- Quanty governance respects policy precedence.
- Compliance references authoritative product records.
- Billing ownership remains in Economy.
- Enterprise integrations have explicit lifecycle/revocation.
- Multi-tenant and policy-boundary testing is defined.
