# M13 — Delivery Rate & Abuse Controls

## Controls

- per-user sending limits
- per-domain limits
- organization limits
- recipient velocity limits
- IP/connection limits
- provider backpressure
- anomaly detection
- temporary suspension

Limits are policy-driven and observable.

## Safety

Rate limits are not only performance controls. They protect:
- account reputation
- recipient safety
- provider relationships
- infrastructure availability

## Escalation

Repeated abuse signals can move an account into restricted sending state.

High-impact enforcement requires auditability and clear recovery paths.
