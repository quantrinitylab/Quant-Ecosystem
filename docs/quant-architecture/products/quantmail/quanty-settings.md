# M10 — Quanty Settings

## User controls

- AI assistance level
- automatic suggestions
- tool permission defaults
- approval strictness
- memory retention policy
- personalization controls
- model/latency preference where supported

## Hard boundaries

User preference cannot grant Quanty capabilities forbidden by platform or product policy.

Examples:
- "auto send everything" cannot remove outbound safety policy.
- "remember everything" cannot override privacy/security retention rules.
- "allow all tools" cannot bypass capability allowlists.

## Transparency

Every consequential preference explains:
- what changes
- what data may be used
- what actions become easier
- what safety controls remain mandatory
