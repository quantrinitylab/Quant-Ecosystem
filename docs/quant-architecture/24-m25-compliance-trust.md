# M25 — QuantMail Compliance, Legal & Trust

## Definition of done
- Compliance controls have owners and evidence.
- Framework mappings are versioned and not confused with certifications.
- DPA/privacy terms have controlled lifecycle.
- Subprocessors have approval and residency governance.
- Legal holds integrate with lifecycle without granting unrestricted access.
- Customer Compliance Center has entitlement boundaries.
- Public Trust Center content is versioned and evidence-backed.
- Compliance evidence exports are scoped and expiring.
- Regulatory/legal requests have controlled workflows.
- Transparency reporting protects customer privacy.
- Compliance exceptions are time-bounded.
- Vendor risk feeds security/privacy/residency decisions.
- Compliance controls are tested and reviewable.
